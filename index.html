FAQ

What founders ask before they apply.

All questions ↗
Will I lose control of my company?+

No. Kuzana takes a non-controlling minority stake. You decide dividends. The partnership is permanent unless you want us to exit.

Do I need perfect accounting or a pitch deck?+

No. We'd rather work with real than perfect.

What's the time commitment?+

Four sessions a month for 12 months: one in-person workshop in Spring Valley, Nairobi, two online sessions, and one 1:1 session at your office.

How long does it take?+

A decision in 2 weeks. Application to investment in about 3 months.

Am I eligible?+

Ksh500k+ monthly revenue, direct sales where you own the customer relationship, usually 3 months to 5 years old, operating in Kenya, and a CEO with the ambition to grow to Ksh100m monthly revenue. Check yourself above.

What do you need from us after you invest?+

Monthly accounts in professional accounting software such as Zoho, closed by the 5th of each month, and separate business and personal bank accounts. None of this is needed to apply.

How does Kuzana make its money back?+

Through revenue-based finance on the working capital we provide, and through services like Lead Gen marketing. Not by selling your shares.

Will you push us to grow fast or make flashy announcements?+

No. This is not a get-rich-quick; it's a long relationship that builds wealth and connection throughout your career.
