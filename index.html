Batch 1 Results 4 Companies Doubled Revenue

When we launched Batch 1, we made a public commitment: if companies didn’t grow by 50% in 6 months, we’d shut down. They didn’t grow 50%—they grew 174% on average. That’s what happens when execution meets the right systems and capital.

1

Avocado / Agri-Export

Raised working capital and set up a Netherlands-registered company — unlocking access to cheaper international debt that was previously unavailable to a Kenya-only structure.

RESULT

Revenue increased 2x. A new export financing channel has opened.

2

HealthFood

Customer retention was 35% monthly meaning 65% of customers left. They rebuilt the retention system, bringing retention to 68% monthly. Accounting systems were put in place that let the founder respond to investor due diligence request within 24 hours, resulting in additional Ksh3.5M in capital.

RESULT

Revenue increased 5x. Customer retention doubled (35% → 68%). Ksh3.5M raised. Revenue trajectory stabilised and grew.

Soy

Identified capital as the primary growth constraint. Structured the business fundraising business to raise Ksh20M in additional capital, in particular, put in place an accounting system outside investors could trust.

RESULT

Revenue increased 4x Ksh20M raised. Production capacity expanded. Ready to scale.

4

Animal Feed

Launched the company's first-ever social media advertising — a channel that had never been tried and paired organic growth with Ksh8M in follow-on capital via introduction.

RESULT

Revenue increased 3x New revenue channel via social media ads. Ksh8M follow-on capital. Investor-ready financial systems.

What You'll Learn at the
Kuzana Entrepreneur Workshops

Each Friday workshop in the Kuzana Investment Program covers one high-impact business topic — taught by operators who have built real companies in Kenya. These are not theory sessions. Every workshop is designed to give founders the CEO mindset, startup systems, and growth strategies they need to double revenue in 12 months.

CEO Mindset for Founders

Learn how to think like a CEO and identify the real bottlenecks in your business. This workshop builds the startup leadership skills founders need to move from operator to owner — developing the CEO mindset and leadership for founders that drives real, sustainable growth.

Branding for Startups

Build a brand that makes your product sell itself and attracts the right customers. This workshop covers startup branding and brand strategy — practical tools to help entrepreneurs build a brand that drives demand and turns a good product into a category leader.

Sales for Entrepreneurs

Turn customers into your best investors by mastering high-conversion sales strategies. This sales training for entrepreneurs covers startup sales from first conversation to close — giving founders the confidence and systems to consistently sell their product.

Customer Experience & Retention

Design systems that drive repeat sales and long-term customer loyalty. This workshop focuses on customer experience strategy and customer retention — building the processes that keep customers coming back and grow revenue without constantly chasing new leads.

Startup Operations & Systems

Map every step of your business to create scalable and efficient operations. This workshop builds the startup systems and business operations frameworks founders need to grow without becoming the bottleneck — and to optimise every process for speed, quality, and scale.

Human Resources for Startups

Build systems for hiring, managing, and retaining a high-performing team. This workshop covers HR for startups — giving founders the hiring systems and team management tools to recruit the right people and build a culture that keeps them.

Public Speaking & Storytelling for Founders

Learn how to tell powerful stories that win customers, investors, and attention. This workshop develops public speaking and storytelling for business — sharpening the pitching skills every founder needs to communicate their vision and get the room on their side.

Finance for Founders

Understand your numbers and steer your business like a captain, not a passenger. This startup finance workshop builds real financial literacy for founders — covering cash flow management, financial decision-making, and the numbers every entrepreneur in Kenya needs to know to grow with confidence.
