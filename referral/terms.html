Kuzana Referral Terms and Conditions

The maximum referral bonus is Ksh50,000 per company that receives investment from Kuzana. The bonus is split into half cash and half stock options of the referred company, paid upon the investee’s successful completion of the acceleration program.

To qualify, the referred company must use the referral contact information in their application. If multiple referrals are submitted for the same company, only the earliest valid referral will be considered eligible for the bonus. Family of direct and indirect decision makers and employees of Kuzana investments and employees/shareholders/contractors of the investee ineligible. Referree, by receiving payment, agrees for Kuzana to announce their name and contribution to the ecosystem for having a great network that resulted in a referral.

This program is subject to change without notice. Bonus payments are subject to applicable taxes and legal obligations. This referral opportunity is not a solicitation for securities and should not be interpreted as such.

The referral program is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

The Kuzana investment program is built for Kenya's most ambitious founders-12 months of entrepreneur workshops, real capital, and the kind of support no other investment company in Kenya offers.
