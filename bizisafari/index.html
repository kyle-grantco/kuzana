Business Safari: Frontier Market Immersion (Kenya)

12 days of high-intensity, field-level learning with founders, factory floors, and investors.

Register Interest
  • Dates: March 22 – April 4, 2026 (12 Days)
  • Investment: $9,500 per participant (Excluding flights, visa, safari)
  • Group Size: Capped at 7 participants
  • Location: Nairobi, rural counties, and Amboseli
  • Host: Kuzana (Led by founder, Kyle Schutter)

Overview

Business Safari is an immersive, expert-guided experience for investors, senior operators, executives, and technical leaders seeking direct exposure to frontier-market innovation in Kenya. It is a field immersion—not a conference—focused on firsthand interactions and practical observation. The program culminates with the Kuzana Final Presentations and a retreat in the Masai Mara.

What You Will Experience

  • 20+ Companies, Real Operations: Curated early- and growth-stage ventures in agriculture, manufacturing, consumer products, and food systems. Expect operational walkthroughs, financial drivers, and founder decision frameworks. Examples: premium steaks, pig feed manufacturing, soybean aggregation, pyrethrum production.
  • Strategic Engagement: Contribute expertise in structured sessions with founders scaling under real constraints.
  • Prison Reform: Explore rule-of-law challenges and rehabilitation pathways inside Kenya’s prison system with a justice-sector innovator.
  • Entrepreneurial & Cultural Landscape: Macro briefings, markets, logistics hubs, and exposure to formal and informal economies.
  • Kuzana Final Presentations (Mar 31): Join investors and leaders at Nairobi Street Kitchen for the capstone presentations of batch3.
  • Amboseli Retreat: Final two days dedicated to wildlife.

Ideal For

Investors, senior operators, executives, technical leaders, impact-aligned professionals, and corporate teams exploring emerging market opportunities and leadership development.
