Underwriting

Selection discipline.

Only the top 7 applicants are accepted per batch. These are the screens every company passes.

01 · RevenueKsh500k+ per month at entry
02 · Gross marginTypically 20–80%
03 · Working capitalShort cycles; no long payment waits on low margins
04 · LeverageNo debt above assets or revenue
05 · Sales channelSells direct and owns the customer (social media, WhatsApp, email, own sales team); not dependent on supermarkets or distributors
06 · FounderA CEO able to scale to Ksh100m monthly revenue
07 · ReportingAfter investment: professional accounting closed by the 5th of each month, and separate business and personal bank accounts
08 · GeographyOperating in Kenya
Structure

Holding period and structure.
