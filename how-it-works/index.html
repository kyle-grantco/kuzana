How The Kuzana Entrepreneur
Education + Investment Program Works

A simple, structured program for founders who are already building, focused on execution, accountability, and real business growth.

Apply

We start with founders who are already building. You don't need a polished pitch deck. You need evidence you're taking action, a real problem you're working on, and the willingness to be challenged. We're not looking for potential; we're looking for motion.

Selection

We select a small group, only 7 companies per batch, who show resourcefulness, speed of learning, focus, and a bias toward action. It's not about the best idea. It's about who is most likely to figure things out.

Foundation First

Before anything else, we get to fundamentals: real revenue fast, a clear business model, and a true understanding of your numbers. If the foundation is weak, nothing else matters.

Build With Structure

Over 12 intensive months, we install the core systems most founders never build: operational rhythms, accounting and financial reporting (real numbers, not guesses), and clear execution priorities. This is where the business becomes real.

Board, Community & Accountability

You don't build alone at Kuzana. Each founder operates within a board-style decision-making structure and a tight community of other serious builders. Better decisions, faster learning, consistent pressure to improve.

Continuous Pressure to Improve

We don't do passive mentorship. We challenge assumptions, push for faster execution, and cut distractions. The goal is simple: help you move faster and make better decisions every single month.

Capital, When It Makes Sense

We believe in fast capital and early honesty. If the business is working, we move quickly. But capital only comes after revenue is proven, the model is clear, and you've demonstrated the ability to execute. Money should amplify a good business, not try to fix a broken one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much equity do you take?

Our investment is $20k cash + $20k in support, so the percentage depends on your valuation. We take a non-controlling minority stake; you remain in charge of your business.

How long does the investment process take?

It usually takes 4–8 weeks from your application to investment, depending on due diligence and final approvals.

Do I need polished accounts or a business plan to apply?

No. We mainly want to understand your business and growth potential. Existing financial records help, but perfection isn't required.

Will you force us to grow fast or make public announcements?

No. We support sustainable growth at a pace that makes sense for your business, and public announcements are never mandatory.

What happens after the 12 months?

After the program, you'll continue running your business independently while remaining part of our founder network for ongoing support.

Can I attend remotely?

Some sessions can be attended remotely, but certain activities may require in-person participation.

Is the Ksh 400k monthly revenue a hard limit?

No. It's a guideline to help identify businesses at the right stage. Exceptional companies are always considered.

Is equity investment Sharia-compliant?

We encourage founders to discuss their specific circumstances with their advisors. Investment structures can vary depending on requirements.
