New to Kuzana?

Kuzana is an education and investment programme for Kenyan entrepreneurs, based in Spring Valley, Nairobi. We invest in a small group of companies at a time and work with their founders every Friday for 12 months on sales, systems, accounting and capital.

Each group is called a batch. Batch 4 is our fourth. The Finale is where they show what changed.

  • $40Kinvested per company in cash and support
  • 12months working with each founder
  • 174%average growth of Batch 1 companies
