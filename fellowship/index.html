Overview

A four-month operating fellowship in Nairobi

For people who want high-leverage experience inside real Kenyan companies — not classroom case studies.

Too much talent is funneled into the "default" careersThe fellowship gives you a chance to change the world while building a career.
Why “Live Players”?Live player (n.)A person, group or guild who steps outside the expected role, rewrites the script and changes reality.Reality is editable. Choose your path.
In honor of the late Prof. Josef Mittlemann

The nudge that changed my life

His $5,000 grant in 2010 caused me, Kyle Schutter, to switch careers from investment banking or a PhD to entrepreneurship.

I started a biogas company in Kenya, then raised $20m in East Africa, eventually leading to Kuzana.

A small nudge at the right moment can change a life.

This fellowship exists to pay it forward.

FAQ

Questions applicants ask

When and where is the fellowship?+

Nairobi, June 1–September 30, 2027. Four months embedded with the Bizi, Kuzana’s portfolio companies, living at The Warren.

Is this full-time?+

Yes. Days are for company operations. Evenings include political economy discussions, workshops, and your Zanzalu project.

Who is it for?+

African and African-diaspora operators aged 18–35+, curious about politics and development, with experience in operations, marketing, manufacturing or business, and ready to work inside real companies.

What is a Live Player?+

A person, group or guild who steps outside the expected role, rewrites the script and changes reality.

What will I actually do day to day?+

Run real operating work: sales follow-up, dashboards, receivables, hiring, social content, or launching new regions — depending on the company. You get real constraints and real feedback.

Which companies might I join?+

Examples include Soya Logistics, Bellfeeds, Foke, Saint Boss, Nawiri, Kaya manufacturing and Paycloud. Final placement depends on fit and company needs.

What support do fellows get?+

Housing at The Warren, stipend, travel to Nairobi, daily Uber to work, mentorship and expert sessions, and a Zanzalu capstone.

How do I apply?+

Apply at form.kuzana.co/fellowship. Questions: info@kuzana.co.
